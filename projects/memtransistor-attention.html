Single-Step Extraction of Transformer Attention With Dual-Gated Memtransistor Crossbars

Nethmi Jayasinghe1 Maeesha Binte Hashem1 Dinithi Jayasuriya1 Leila Rahimifard1 Min-A Kang2 Vinod K. Sangwan2 Mark C. Hersam2 Amit Ranjan Trivedi1

1University of Illinois Chicago (ECE)  ·  2Northwestern University

Paper (PDF) DOI / IEEE Xplore BibTeX
TL;DR

A hardware–software co-design that accelerates Transformer attention: attention scores are extracted in a single step, directly from input tokens, with no intermediate query/key vectors - at 2.37× lower energy and less than half the compute-in-memory cells.

Abstract

We discuss how a dual-gated memtransistor crossbar can accelerate the extraction of the Transformer's attention scores. A memtransistor is a novel two-dimensional material-based device that offers non-volatile programmability and gate tunability.

Leveraging these attributes, we demonstrate the extraction of quadratic-order products on a single memtransistor and the single-step extraction of attention scores without inferring intermediate query/key vectors. The query/key-free processing of memtransistor-based attention scoring results in 2.37× lower energy with less than half the crossbar cells.
