About

I am a PhD student in Electrical & Computer Engineering at the University of Illinois Chicago, specializing in reinforcement learning, adaptive decision-making, and robust AI systems. My research focuses on adaptive agents that make real-time decisions under distribution shifts while maintaining stable behavior - recovering from faults and unexpected dynamics without retraining, with applications across robotic platforms. Recent work was accepted to ICML 2026.

I build and evaluate these systems in PyTorch with rigorous experimentation pipelines, and I am broadly interested in representation learning, multi-modal reasoning, and sequential decision-making for real-world interactive systems. I also draw on brain-inspired principles to make learning faster, more stable, and more efficient.
